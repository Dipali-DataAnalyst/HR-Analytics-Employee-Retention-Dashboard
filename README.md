# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built in Microsoft Excel to analyze employee workforce data, attrition, job satisfaction, compensation, experience, work-life balance, and departmental trends.

This project demonstrates how raw HR data can be transformed into meaningful KPIs, pivot-table analysis, and an interactive dashboard to support data-driven HR decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze employee data and identify important HR trends and patterns related to:

* Employee attrition
* Department-wise workforce distribution
* Employee satisfaction
* Job roles
* Employee experience
* Monthly income
* Work-life balance
* Gender distribution
* Years since last promotion
* Workforce demographics

The dashboard provides a centralized view of important HR metrics and helps HR teams identify areas that may require further investigation.

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard focuses on several important HR dimensions.

### 1. Employee Attrition

Attrition is analyzed across departments and employee characteristics to understand where employee turnover may be higher.

### 2. Department Analysis

Employee distribution is analyzed across departments such as:

* Hardware
* Human Resources
* Research & Development
* Sales
* Software

This helps understand workforce composition across different business areas.

### 3. Job Role Analysis

The dataset contains multiple job roles, allowing comparison of workforce characteristics and employee experience across different positions.

### 4. Compensation Analysis

Monthly income and hourly rate are analyzed to understand compensation patterns across departments, job roles, gender, and attrition status.

### 5. Experience Analysis

The dashboard considers:

* Total Working Years
* Years at Company
* Years in Current Role
* Years Since Last Promotion
* Years with Current Manager

These metrics can help identify employee career progression and experience patterns.

### 6. Work-Life Balance

Employee work-life balance is analyzed across different job roles to identify workforce patterns and potential HR concerns.

### 7. Employee Satisfaction

Job satisfaction and satisfaction-related metrics are included to understand employee experience.

### 8. Demographic Analysis

The dashboard also provides insights into employee demographics including gender and marital status.

## 🔍 Sample Findings

Some of the notable observations from the analysis include:

* The dataset contains approximately **50,000 employee records**.
* Workforce size varies across departments, with Sales, Software, Research & Development, Human Resources, and Hardware represented in the analysis.
* Attrition can be compared across departments to identify areas with different turnover patterns.
* Employee satisfaction can be analyzed by gender.
* Work-life balance varies across different job roles.
* Average working experience can be compared across departments.
* Employee compensation can be analyzed together with attrition status.
* Marital-status-based workforce distribution provides an additional demographic perspective.

> These findings are intended as exploratory HR insights rather than definitive explanations for employee behavior.
